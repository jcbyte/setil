# Privacy Policy for Setil

**Last Updated:** 11/08/2026

Setil is an open-source application designed for cost splitting. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you use the app.

### 1. Information We Collect

When you use Setil, we process the following minimum data required for app functionality:

- **Account Information:** When you sign in with your Google Account, we receive your basic authentication details (such as your email address, display name, and profile picture URL) provided by Google Firebase Authentication.
- **Photos and Images:** If you choose to upload a custom profile picture or group avatar, the image file is uploaded and hosted via Cloudinary.
- **User-Generated Data:** Expense details, groups, balances, and cost-splitting transactions you create within the app are stored in our Firebase Database (Firestore).
- **Device Identifiers & Push Tokens:** If you enable push notifications, we collect unique Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens generated by your device to send real-time notification alerts regarding group activity and balance updates.

### 2. How Your Data Is Used

- To authenticate your identity and synchronize your groups, expenses, and balances across devices.
- To display profile avatars and deliver push notifications for group updates.
- We **do not** track your activity, run advertisements, or profile you for marketing.
- We **do not** sell, trade, or rent your personal information or cost-splitting data to third parties.

### 3. Third-Party Services

The app relies on the following third-party infrastructure service providers to function:

- **Firebase / Google Cloud:** Handles user authentication, secure cloud database storage (Firestore), and push notification delivery (Firebase Cloud Messaging). Please review [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details on their security practices.
- **Cloudinary:** Acts as a content delivery network and cloud media host for user-uploaded profile and avatar images. Please review [Cloudinary's Privacy Policy](https://cloudinary.com/privacy) for more information.

### 4. Open Source Transparency

Setil is open-source. You can inspect the source code and verify our data handling practices on GitHub at: [github.com/jcbyte/setil/](https://github.com/jcbyte/setil)

### 5. Data Deletion & Account Requests

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and personal information at any time.

- **Personal Data Erased:** Your authentication credentials, email address, profile pictures (hosted on Cloudinary), push notification tokens, and direct account settings will be permanently deleted from our servers.
- **Shared Ledger Data:** To maintain financial and balance integrity for remaining group members, historical transaction entries created by you will be anonymised (disassociated from your identity) rather than deleted.
- **How to Request:** To request account deletion, contact the developer (Joel Cutler) directly via email at joelcutler108@gmail.com.
